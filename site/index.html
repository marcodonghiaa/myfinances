Set up in about 30 minutes

On free plans. No GitHub account, nothing left running on your computer.

  1. Create free accountsSupabase, Enable Banking and Vercel. A Gemini key is optional.
  2. Run one commandnpx create-alopex on Mac, Linux or Windows (Node 20+). It sets up the database and sync, and prints your dashboard URL.
  3. Link a bankFrom the Accounts page. Balances appear at once, transactions with the next sync.

Frequently asked questions

What is Alopex?

Alopex is a free, open-source (MIT) net worth tracker for people with European bank accounts. It syncs balances and transactions read-only from 2000+ banks over PSD2, prices your stocks and crypto, and keeps everything in your own Supabase database. You install it with one command: npx create-alopex.

Is there an open-source alternative to Monarch Money or Finary?

Yes. Alopex is MIT-licensed and self-hosted: European bank accounts, stocks, ETFs and crypto in one dashboard, with the data in your own Supabase database. Monarch Money costs $99.99 a year and connects US and Canadian banks. Finary is free for 2 accounts and paid from €54.99 a year.

How is Alopex different from Firefly III, Actual Budget or Ghostfolio?

Firefly III and Actual Budget are budgeting apps. Ghostfolio tracks investments. Alopex adds up bank accounts, investments and crypto into one net worth, with European bank sync built in.

Is Alopex free? What's the catch?

Yes. The code is free under the MIT license, and it runs on the free plans of Supabase, Vercel and Enable Banking (their limits can change). The catch: you run it yourself. Setup takes about 30 minutes, you renew bank access every 180 days, and you update by running the same command again.

Which banks does Alopex support, and how long does access last?

Any of the 2000+ banks on Enable Banking, including Revolut, Wise and Fineco. Bank access lasts 180 days under PSD2 (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/2360). You get a reminder before it ends, and renewing keeps your history.

Do I need a server to run Alopex?

No. The sync runs on a schedule inside your Supabase project, and the dashboard runs on Vercel. No GitHub account is needed. On a home server, use Docker.

Who is Alopex not for?

People who bank outside Europe (bank sync works only with EU-regulated banks), need real-time balances (sync runs every 6 hours), want budgets or forecasts (not built yet), or want broker sync (only Coinbase syncs; you add other holdings by ticker or ISIN).

Can Alopex move money or make payments?

No. Bank access under PSD2 and the Coinbase key are both read-only.
