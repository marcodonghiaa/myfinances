Where your data goes

Four hops. The last two run under your own accounts, and nothing in this line is ours.

Your bank

Balances and transactions

Released under PSD2 account-information access. Read-only by law.

Enable Banking

Licensed relay

Fetches from the bank and hands the data on. Keeps no copy for Alopex.

Your Supabase

Postgres, your account

Row Level Security on every table. The service-role key never reaches the browser.

Your Vercel

The dashboard

Deployed from your fork. Updates arrive as pull requests you merge.

  • Optional: Google Gemini, only if you add a key, receives transaction descriptions for categorization.
  • Sync logs hold counts, never names or amounts. Alopex cannot move money.

Set up in about 30 minutes

On free tiers. Nothing to keep running on your computer after setup.

  1. Create free accountsSupabase, Enable Banking and Vercel. Optional Gemini key. No GitHub needed.
  2. Run one commandnpx create-alopex on Mac, Linux or Windows (Node 20+). It builds the database, deploys the functions and schedules the sync.
  3. Get your dashboardThe same command puts it on Vercel and prints your URL. Sign in with the login you chose.
  4. Link a bankFrom the Accounts page. Balances appear at once, transactions with the next sync.

Who it is not for

You bank outside Europe. PSD2 only. No Plaid, SimpleFIN or US banks.

You want someone else to run it. You host it, merge updates and renew bank consent every 180 days.

You want real-time balances. Sync runs every 6 hours; banks cap unattended access at about 4 calls a day.

You want budgets, goals or forecasts. Not today.

You want broker sync. You enter holdings; only Coinbase syncs balances.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an open-source alternative to Monarch Money or Finary?

Yes. Alopex is MIT-licensed and self-hosted: European bank accounts, stocks, ETFs and crypto in one dashboard, with the data in your own Supabase project.

How is Alopex different from Firefly III, Actual Budget or Ghostfolio?

Firefly III and Actual budget; Ghostfolio tracks investments. Alopex does net worth across bank accounts, investments and crypto, with EU bank sync built in.

Is Alopex free?

Yes, MIT. The default setup runs on free tiers of GitHub, Supabase, Vercel and Enable Banking, whose limits can change.

Which banks does Alopex support, and how long does access last?

Any of the 2000+ on Enable Banking, including Revolut, Wise and Fineco. Consent lasts 180 days; you get a reminder before it ends and renew without losing history.

Do I need a server to run Alopex?

No. Sync runs on a schedule inside your Supabase project and the dashboard on Vercel. Docker is an option for a home server.

Can Alopex move money or make payments?

No. PSD2 account-information access and the Coinbase key are both read-only.
