Alopex / Integrations

Integrations

Alopex connects to 2000+ European banks through Enable Banking, to Coinbase with a read-only key, and imports Splitwise, CSV and PDF files. Everything runs in your own Supabase and Vercel accounts, or on your own server with Docker. Every connection is read-only: Alopex can never move money.

Updated 10 November 2026

Banks

Enable Banking

Read-only bank sync for 2000+ banks in 31 European countries, over PSD2.

Investments and crypto

Coinbase

Your Coinbase balances in your net worth, with a read-only API key.

Shared costs

Splitwise

Your share of group expenses, and who owes you, from a Splitwise export.

AI

Google Gemini

Optional AI categories and subscription detection, with your own key.

Database and sync

Supabase

Your own database. The sync runs inside it on a schedule.

Hosting

Vercel

Your dashboard on your own Vercel account, deployed by the installer.

Import

CSV and PDF import

Bring past net worth, bank statements and trades from CSV or PDF.

Hosting

Docker

Run the sync and the dashboard on your own computer or home server.
