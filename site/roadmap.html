Roadmap

What we are building next for Alopex, the open-source net worth tracker for European banks. Vote for what matters to you, or suggest something new: every idea is a public GitHub issue, and you get notified when it ships.

Updated 10 November 2026

In development

  • More brokersInvestmentsTarget Dec 2026👍 Vote ↗

    Sync for more brokers and exchanges, starting with those that offer a read-only API.

  • Splitwise syncShared costsTarget Dec 2026👍 Vote ↗

    For Splitwise Pro users: your groups synced through the Splitwise API, no more exporting files.

Next

  • Your own base currencyMoneyTarget Dec 2026👍 Vote ↗

    Net worth and spending in your home currency, for non-euro countries like Poland, Sweden or Czechia.

  • Credit cards and debtsAccountsTarget Dec 2026👍 Vote ↗

    Mark an account as a credit card, loan or debt, so it counts against your net worth.

  • Catch your free trialsSubscriptionsTarget Jan 2027👍 Vote ↗

    A push before a free trial turns into a paid subscription.

  • BudgetsSpendingTarget Jan 2027👍 Vote ↗

    A monthly limit per category, and how far into it you are.

  • Every number shows its transactionsSpendingTarget Dec 2026👍 Vote ↗

    Tap any total and see the exact transactions behind it.

  • AI agent that cancels subscriptionsSubscriptionsTarget Feb 2027👍 Vote ↗

    Pick a subscription you no longer want, and an AI agent takes care of cancelling it for you.

  • AI chat about your moneyPlanningTarget Feb 2027👍 Vote ↗

    Ask questions about your own spending and plan ahead. It explains your numbers; it is not financial advice.

Targets are our best estimate and can move. Every change that ships is in the changelog.
